About

I've spent the last five years on the operational side of e-commerce — the part that keeps a storefront running once the product itself is ready. That's meant managing Amazon Seller Central and Vendor Central accounts end to end for clients based outside India, working entirely remotely across time zones.

Day to day, that's looked like researching what to list and how to word it, keeping campaigns and inventory in shape, and being the person customers actually hear back from. I also set up and maintain storefronts on Shopify and WordPress for clients who sell beyond Amazon alone.

What I do

Listings & catalog

Product and keyword research, listing creation and optimization, A+ content, and catalog upkeep so pages actually convert.

Advertising

Planning and running Sponsored Products, Brands, and Display campaigns, with daily sales tracking to see what's working.

Inventory & fulfillment

FBA and FBM management — stock monitoring, planning, and order coordination so accounts don't run into stranded or out-of-stock issues.

Customer support

Handling buyer messages and queries directly, protecting account health and keeping response times where they need to be.

Storefronts

Setting up and maintaining Shopify and WordPress sites for clients selling across more than one channel.
